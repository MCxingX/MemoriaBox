# Product

## Register

product

## Users

念记 is used by Android users who want to keep dates, birthdays, todos, diary notes, photos, and reminders in one private daily tool. They use it in short sessions, often on varied Android devices, screen sizes, and font-scale settings.

## Product Purpose

念记 helps users preserve meaningful days and act on upcoming reminders with local-first storage, backups, calendar views, widgets, and lightweight organization. Success means users can find, review, edit, back up, and restore personal memories without losing data or fighting the interface.

## Brand Personality

Gentle, minimal, tidy. The interface should feel warm and emotionally calm while staying practical, readable, and familiar as a Material 3 Android product.

## Anti-references

Avoid loud decorative UI, crowded card grids, low-contrast pastel text, fixed-height layouts that break on Android font scaling, and generic gradient-heavy visual treatments. Avoid workflows that hide destructive or data-changing actions behind unclear copy.

## Design Principles

- Preserve personal data first: backup, import, and restore flows should make data safety obvious.
- Keep daily tasks calm: prioritize clear hierarchy, short labels, and predictable Material 3 controls.
- Support varied devices: layouts should adapt to screen width, density, dynamic text size, and long Chinese strings.
- Use warmth with restraint: soft color and shape can support emotional tone while primary actions stay unmistakable.
- Make empty and error states useful: every blank, failed, or loading state should tell the user what happened and what action is available.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG AA contrast, readable typography, clear focus and touch targets, reduced-motion-friendly behavior, dynamic font scaling, and multi-resolution Android compatibility.
